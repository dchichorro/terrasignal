From alert to qualified lead in one screen

1

Demand

Disaster alerts from GDACS, USGS, NASA EONET and Copernicus EMS activations are merged and de-duplicated. Every event gets a demand score from hazard type, alert level, magnitude and recency. An EMS activation counts as confirmed, funded demand.

2

Supply

For each event we query the real Sentinel-2 optical and Sentinel-1 radar catalogues: freshness, cloud and revisit. Live orbits tell you when the next free pass is due.

3

Gap → lead

Demand splits into a coverage gap, a VHR gap (sub-metre needs) and free-serviceable work. Each event gets the product that closes it and an indicative deal value.

demand = coverage gap + VHR gap + free-serviceable

Transparent and unit-tested. Free supply S blends optical and radar (SAR counts more for floods and quakes, less for burn severity). The VHR share is set per hazard: free 10 m pixels can't grade building damage, however fresh they are.
